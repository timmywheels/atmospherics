## Description (4000)

```
Atmospherics takes you somewhere else, by sound.

Ask for a log cabin in a snowstorm, a rainy night in the city or dusk at the lake, and you're there: the fire crackling, the storm against the walls, loons calling across still water.

TELEPORT
Build a scene as a sentence: take me to a tent, in a thunderstorm, at night. Or just type it your own way. Seventeen settings, from a sleeper train to a temple garden, in any weather, at any hour.

REAL WEATHER, SOMEWHERE REAL
When it's really snowing somewhere that fits, your cabin takes that place's weather, live: "Snowing in Lofoten right now, −3°". The rest of the time the scene is built for the hour you chose, with the birds, insects and weather that belong to it.

INSIDE, OUT OF THE RAIN
Indoors, the walls soften the rain and the wind, while the fire, the café or the train keep going all night.

A SHAPE OF LIGHT
Every soundscape is a glowing glass form, one light for each sound, swelling toward the loudest. Turn it with your finger, or tap it to see its topology.

TOPOLOGY
Every sound is a hill on a map around you, as high as it is loud. Drag one closer to hear more of it, or off the edge to let it go. Add rain, a fire, birds, wind, thunder, the ocean: more than 50 sounds in all.

OUTSIDE
Tap Outside to hear the weather at your own window, as it changes through the night.

LIBRARY
Ready-made mixes for sleep, focus and calm, with the research behind the focus ones. Save your own, too.

MADE FOR SLEEP
• Sleep timer up to 8 hours
• Keeps playing with the screen locked, with lock screen controls
• Widgets and a Live Activity
• Swipe to travel, swipe down to change the weather, swipe up for your library

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Atmospherics only uses your approximate location to play the weather outside, and everything else works without it.

Weather by Open-Meteo.com.
```
