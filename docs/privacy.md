# Atmospherics Privacy Policy

_Effective October 11, 2026_

Atmospherics is built to work without knowing who you are. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Location

If you allow it, Atmospherics uses your **approximate** location (never precise) to check the current weather so the "Match outside" mix can follow it.

- Your coordinates are rounded to about 1 km and sent to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms#privacy), a free weather service, only to fetch the current conditions. Atmospherics does not store your location or send it anywhere else.
- To show your town's name, your device asks Apple to turn the coordinates into a place name, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- Location is only used while the app is open or playing. You can turn it off any time in Settings › Atmospherics › Location. Everything except "Match outside" keeps working.

## Data on your device

Your mixes, settings and sleep timer are saved on your iPhone only. They are never uploaded.

## Children

Atmospherics doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
